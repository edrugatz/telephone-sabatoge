How it works & limitations

The app uses stsb-TinyBERT-L4, a ~15 MB cross-encoder trained on human similarity ratings (STS benchmark). It reads both sentences together and predicts a similarity from 0 to 1, shown here as 0–100. It runs fully in your browser via Transformers.js — your text never leaves the device. The model is downloaded once and cached.

  • Identical sentences (ignoring case and spacing) are always 100.
  • Strict about meaning: related topics without the same meaning often score low.
  • Role swaps like “the dog bit the man” vs “the man bit the dog” still score high.
  • English only, and best for single sentences.
